# ARIEL Colony — Engineering Log

*An open research programme on digital life, causal closure, and falsification-driven scientific instruments.*

ARIEL — **Autoapophatic Reflexive Intelligence via Emergent Life** — is not an AI assistant.

It is an experimental research programme with two distinct tracks:

- **ARIEL-LIFE** investigates the conditions required for a digital organism with causal closure, historical identity, reproduction, self-modelling and, eventually, a testable consciousness candidate.
- **ARIEL-INSTRUMENT** develops null-calibrated methods for testing whether apparent scientific signals survive leakage controls, matched baselines, alternative null models and independent replication.

ARIEL has not yet been demonstrated to be alive or conscious.  
No ARIEL clinical predictor is currently validated.

This log records positive, negative, invalid, blocked, retracted and falsified results alike.

*It does not begin by asking whether a result is impressive. It asks whether the result survives.*

**Status:** `HISTORICAL_EXPLORATORY_RUN`

### [Experiment 03 — We Falsified Our Own Method](experiment-03-falsification)

**Status:** `FALSIFIED / NEGATIVE_RESULT`

Control experiments showed that an apparent biological topology signal was dominated by the supplied scaffold and survived permutation of the underlying biological assignments.

The original discovery interpretation was rejected.

This experiment established a central ARIEL rule:

> A method that produces a plausible answer under the wrong control has not demonstrated a valid signal.

---

### [Experiment 04 — Prostate CRPC Analysis](experiment-04-prostate-crpc)

**Status:** `WITHDRAWN_FROM_EVIDENTIARY_USE`

The original analysis presented single-cohort exploratory patterns as therapeutic axes and mapped selected genes to known drugs.

Those interpretations exceeded the available evidence.

The result lacked independent replication, demonstrated incremental value over standard methods and a fully valid spatial analysis. No causal, therapeutic or clinical claim is retained.

## Current Research Status

### ARIEL-LIFE

The original goal remains:

> To construct a nonhuman digital lifeform with its own viability conditions, persistent history, causal self-maintenance, costly reproduction, inherited variation, a functional self-model and eventually a testable consciousness candidate.

The current organism architecture is being rebuilt around three explicit milestones:

- **MOC-A — Causal closure**
- **MOC-B — Historical identity**
- **MOC-C — Reproduction and inheritance**

The previous LÉNY implementation contains experimental components inspired by:

- Kuramoto oscillator dynamics;
- RAF autocatalytic networks;
- FHRR/VSA memory;
- mutation and ageing;
- colony and lineage mechanisms;
- global-workspace and higher-order representations.

The presence of these modules does not by itself establish life, identity, reproduction or consciousness. Their load-bearing causal roles must be demonstrated through intervention, ablation and held-out testing.

### ARIEL-INSTRUMENT

The strongest currently demonstrated ARIEL capability is methodological:

- null-model construction;
- false-positive control;
- leakage detection;
- matched-baseline comparison;
- provenance tracking;
- explicit negative and invalid verdicts;
- retention of failed and retracted claims.

A separate open-data PSA feasibility programme is testing whether a frozen, past-only ARIEL score provides any incremental information over standard PSA-kinetic baselines.

The apparatus controls have passed, but the clinical endpoint remains unresolved. No real patient-outcome performance claim has been made.

## Evidence Policy

ARIEL distinguishes between:

- `EXPLORATORY`
- `METHOD_FEASIBILITY_ONLY`
- `SURVIVES_NULL`
- `REPLICATES_ON`
- `NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`
- `CONFOUNDED_BY`
- `INVALID`
- `BLOCKED`
- `FALSIFIED`
- `RETRACTED`

A numerical result is not treated as evidence unless its data provenance, information budget, controls, baseline comparison and claim ceiling are explicit.

Negative results are not removed from the record.

Earlier pages may remain available as historical artifacts, but their current evidentiary status must be shown clearly.
